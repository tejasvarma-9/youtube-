# Channel description (paste into YouTube Studio > Customization > Profile > Description)

Who Pays Who follows the money behind the businesses you use every day.

Every video takes one company or industry and answers a simple question: when you pay, who actually gets paid, and how does the business really make its money? Gyms, airlines, casinos, banks, streaming services, vending machines and more.

We work from company filings and reputable sources, run the numbers ourselves, and say out loud when a figure is an estimate. Sources are listed under every video.

New breakdowns every week.

For education and entertainment only. Not financial, investment, legal or tax advice.
