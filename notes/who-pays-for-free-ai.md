THE ANGLE (our read, say it once as "one way to read this"):
"Free AI" is paid for by four different wallets, and which one depends on whose AI you open. (1) An ads business elsewhere in the company: Google and Meta earn most of their money from ads and give their assistants away. (2) Investors: the independent labs raise tens of billions of dollars at a time. (3) A minority of paying subscribers and business customers. (4) You, in two ways some companies use: ads shown inside the free chatbot, and your conversations used to improve the models unless you switch it off. Under all of it is a hardware bill measured in tens of billions of dollars per quarter.

OPENING IDEA (puzzle that withholds the answer, per voice.md): ChatGPT says it has more than 900 million weekly users. It says more than 50 million people subscribe. So who pays for everyone else? Ask within the first 90 words, then the one welcome line, then reveal "four different wallets" at about 60 to 90 seconds. No roadmap.

B. WHAT THE SOURCES DO NOT SAY (the script must be honest about this)
- No company publishes what one free chat costs it in dollars. Do not invent a cost per question.
- No company says in so many words "paying users subsidise free users". Say "one way to read this" when connecting the two.
- Most of the hardware spending also serves paying cloud and business customers, not only free chatbots. Say so once.
- The gains Amazon and Microsoft book on their AI stakes are mostly paper gains on value, not cash coming in.
- Some labs (Anthropic's reported profit, DeepSeek's claimed margins) push back on "it all loses money"; do not claim every AI company loses money.
