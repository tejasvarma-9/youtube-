Angle (our read, say it once as "one way to read this"): the free tier is not a product Spotify sells to listeners. It is the top of the funnel for Premium, and Premium subscribers pay for almost everything.

Figures to use, each to be checked against the primary source:
1. Premium share of total revenue: about 90.7% (Q2 2026, Spotify shareholder deck). Ads are the rest.
2. Free users are about 63.6% of monthly active users but bring about 9.3% of revenue.
3. Ad revenue per free monthly user: about EUR 0.30 a month. Premium revenue per user: about EUR 4.89 a month (Q2 2026, ARPU as Spotify defines it).
4. Cost of revenue is about 66.6% of revenue (this is mostly royalties to labels, publishers and rights holders). Operating margin about 13.7%. Say "mostly royalties" only if the filing says so.
5. US Individual plan price is about 30% higher than in 2023. Confirm start and end prices from Spotify's own price announcements. Do not state a "per song" or "per stream" payout figure unless Spotify's Loud & Clear page gives it.

Illustration allowed, labelled "a rough illustration, not Spotify's own split": if a subscriber pays a US monthly price, about two-thirds of the revenue goes out as royalties and costs (from item 4). Use 2/3, do not invent a cent-by-cent split.

Do not use: layoffs numbers, artist-pay-per-stream numbers, any Joe Rogan deal figure, any claim that Spotify "never made a profit" (say only what the filings show about profitability, and name the year if used).
